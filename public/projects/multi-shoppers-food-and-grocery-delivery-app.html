06 · Food & grocery

Multi-Shoppers Food & Grocery Delivery App

The brief

Help local shops sell online and run their digital stores, from the first order to the doorstep.

Web, Mobile & SaaSE-commerce

What we built

It is a cross-platform application developed using Flutter for user-facing apps and ReactJS for the admin panel. The platform is designed to empower local shop vendors by enabling them to sell their products online and manage their digital stores efficiently. It provides a seamless experience for both customers and shop owners, offering features that facilitate easy product listing, inventory management, and customer interaction.

  1. 01Buy Grocery, Household Items, & Restaurant’s food
  2. 02User App, Seller App, Rider (Delivery Person) App
  3. 03Customer & Delivery Management, Account Management
  4. 04Complete Control of Digital Store, Delivery Areas Setting

The outcome

A seamless experience for customers, with local shop owners in charge of their own stores.
